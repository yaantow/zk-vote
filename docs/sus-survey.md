## SUS Scoring Reference (for manual analysis)

After collecting responses, calculate each participant's SUS score:

- **Odd questions** (Q1, Q3, Q5, Q7, Q9): contribution = response − 1
- **Even questions** (Q2, Q4, Q6, Q8, Q10): contribution = 5 − response
- **Final score** = sum of all 10 contributions × 2.5 → range: 0–100

| Score range | Grade | Label              |
|-------------|-------|--------------------|
| 84.1 – 100  | A+    | Best Imaginable    |
| 80.8 – 84.0 | A     | Excellent          |
| 71.4 – 80.7 | B     | Good               |
| 62.7 – 71.3 | C     | OK                 |
| 51.7 – 62.6 | D     | Poor               |
| 0 – 51.6    | F     | Awful              |

**Industry average SUS score: 68.** A score above 68 is considered above average.
